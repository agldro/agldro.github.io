An “empty” newspaper and a performance about relationship with the news.

In Spatial Newspaper, space is the message. Space is the performance. Space is safety. Space is a tool to esteem. The Spatial Newspaper sets the context for the information to respect it. There is no sound, rush, or normality of life to contradict the terror of news.
