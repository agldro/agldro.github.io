System Error. Product Design Arnhem Graduation Exhibition 2025.

What if the systems we live within - social, technological, political - aren’t working the way they should? What if the glitches and breakdowns are trying to tell us something important? The identity of the graduation exhibition and the catalogue design was based on the metaphor of a desire path, which holds the potential to develop and transform the predefined systems around us.
