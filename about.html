Aglaya Drobysheva

I am a third-year graphic design student at ArtEZ University of the Arts, Arnhem, the Netherlands. Originally from Moscow, Russia.

I am mainly interested in book and editorial design based on research processes and storytelling. I am fascinated by the idea of working with archiving, curating, and exhibiting. In my work, I cherish intertextuality, small gestures, subtlety, visualization of feelings that cannot be put into words, and the process of revealing hidden layers of a bigger social/historical/political context.
