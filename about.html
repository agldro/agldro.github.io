Aglaya Drobysheva is a graphic designer based in Arnhem, the Netherlands.

She is interested in editorial design based on research processes and storytelling, and is fascinated by archiving, curating, and exhibiting various content. In her work, she cherishes intertextuality, small gestures, subtlety, visualization of feelings that cannot be put into words, and the process of revealing hidden layers of bigger social/historical/political contexts.

Aglaya Drobysheva is part of amphi-graph studio, a collabroration with Tabitha Anderton.
