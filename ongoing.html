The Ongoing

Video

May 2023

The Ongoing is a conversation where people endlessly answer each other's videos. The conversation consists of 'stitched' TikTok videos, where some stitches are made in the original videos, and others are created by the designer. The final video creates a loop where the conversation goes from one theme to other while constantly changing direction and being repeated. The discussion does not solve any existing issues or lead to real answers but cultivates hate, and misunderstandings, leaving an overwhelming trace behind.
